## Potential features
Based on the app's purpose as a backpacking trip planning tool, here are some feature suggestions for the dashboard that could enhance the user experience:

1. Trip Statistics Widget
- Show total number of trips planned
- Display upcoming trips countdown
- Show total miles planned across all trips
- Display favorite/ most visited locations
- Show trip completion rate (completed vs planned)

2. Gear Summary Widget
- Display most commonly used items across trips
- Show gear that needs attention (items marked as needing replacement/repair)
- Quick access to frequently used gear lists
- Gear weight statistics across trips
- Seasonal gear recommendations based on upcoming trips

3. Weather & Conditions Widget
- Weather forecast for upcoming trips
- Historical weather data for planned destinations
- Trail conditions updates
- Sunrise/sunset times for trip dates
- Temperature ranges for planned locations

4. Trip Timeline Widget
- Visual timeline of upcoming trips
- Trip preparation checklist
- Countdown to next trip
- Important trip dates (permits, reservations, etc.)
- Trip milestones and achievements

5. Social Features
- Recent activity from trip members
- Trip planning discussions
- Gear recommendations from friends
- Trip photos from previous adventures
- Trip reports and reviews

6. Quick Actions
- Create new trip
- Add gear to pack
- Share trip details
- Export trip data
- Quick access to frequently used features

7. Trip Insights
- Pack weight trends
- Most common gear combinations
- Trip duration patterns
- Seasonal trip patterns
- Favorite gear brands

8. Planning Tools
- Trip budget tracker
- Food planning calculator
- Mileage calculator
- Elevation profile viewer
- Resupply point planner

9. Safety & Emergency
- Emergency contact information
- Trip itinerary sharing
- Weather alerts
- Trail closure notifications
- Safety checklist

10. Integration Features
- Import routes from popular hiking apps
- Export to GPS devices
- Sync with fitness tracking apps
- Share to social media
- Calendar integration
